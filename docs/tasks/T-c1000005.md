## Goal
Give schema preparation one owner, add a compare-and-swap run save primitive, and bound terminal-run growth in state.db.

## Evidence
From T-5115e3a5: schema preparation is a check-then-DDL sequence that two openers can run. From T-baadf270: the live state.db is 8.2 MB after four days with 685 runs, 12,566 decisions and 3,188 run_events. PruneStale deletes deliveries, approvals, decisions and run_events older than 30 days but never deletes runs. 674 of 685 runs are terminal, about 128 MB a year. Age pruning leaves a high-water mark because page_count stays at 164 with freelist_count 57 after a prune.

## Steps
Move schema preparation under one startup owner or a transactional migration guard. Add a compare-and-swap run save primitive keyed by updated_at or head SHA for the reconciler and control callers. Add a bounded terminal-run pass to PruneStale that keeps the newest 20,000 merged, closed or dropped runs and deletes older ones in 1000 row batches. Check base_incidents.fixer_run_id before deleting a run. Evaluate VACUUM or incremental_vacuum with a measured tradeoff instead of enabling it blindly. Add tests in the new internal/infra/sqlite/retention_test.go for the retention boundary and the CAS primitive.

## Accept
Two openers cannot race schema DDL, disk growth stops at a bounded high-water mark, snapshot latency is unchanged, and no fixer incident points at a deleted run.
