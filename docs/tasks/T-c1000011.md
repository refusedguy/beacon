## Goal
Ensure every skip produced by the planner tells the caller what to do next.

## Evidence
From T-7c7f30f9: failures and skips carry no next action. SkippedTask explains why a candidate was left out of a dispatch but gives the caller no instruction.

## Steps
Add a Next field to SkippedTask and set it for each skip code, including active run, active and selected scope overlap, no scope, oversized scope and bad status. Add tests in the new internal/app/planner_next_test.go that every skip carries a non-empty next.

## Accept
No skip reaches the caller without a next action.
