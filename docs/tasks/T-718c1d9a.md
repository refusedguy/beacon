Evidence: opt-app.md shows writeMu is held across the retry loop with 10ms and 20ms sleeps.

Steps: Narrow the lock to the state that truly needs it, or move the retry outside. Preserve busy-error semantics and the narrow run-store ports.

Check: A benchmark with 100 goroutines and one SQLITE_BUSY retry reports lower p95 latency with identical final states and zero errors.
