Goal: A behind PR with a failing required check produces needs_agent_fix on the first observation.

Evidence: cicd-05 and cicd-10 show four rebase attempts and one fix attempt on a small PR. Rebases consume the shared attempt budget before the real lint failure is addressed.

Steps: Make a failing required check win over Behind. Do not rebase while required checks are pending. Carry the failed check name into the decision reason and wake context. Give rebases a small separate budget.

Check: A behind PR with one failing required check returns VerdictNeedsAgentFix. A behind PR with pending checks produces no UpdateBranch call across three ticks.

Accept: No policy or wire change.

Open a PR, call beacon_watch_pr, do not self-merge.
